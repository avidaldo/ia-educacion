## They are increasingly alike

The difference lies in quality, the free plan, integration with your tools and how they treat your data.

Notas:
Models and plans change every few weeks. The page has the review date; the criteria and the settings last longer than the names of the models.

---

<!-- .slide: id="lo-que-ofrecen" class="fragments" -->
## What almost all of them offer

- Reasoning before answering.
- Searching the web and citing sources.
- Working with attached files.
- Voice, camera and screen on a phone.
- Standing instructions, memory and projects.
- Deep research (Deep Research).

<!-- .slide: id="para-que" class="table dense" -->
## Which one to use for what

| If you need… | Use… |
| --- | --- |
| Current information with sources | Perplexity, or any of them with search |
| A report on a new topic | Deep research in any of the big ones |
| To work only with your own documents | A Gemini notebook or a project |
| To create a web page or an application | Claude (Artifacts), ChatGPT or Gemini (Canvas) |
| To generate or edit images | ChatGPT or Gemini |

Notas:
**Demo (5 min): the same request in two chatbots**. Two chatbots open at the same time (for example, ChatGPT and Gemini), in temporary chats.

1. Paste the same request, about the subject of someone in the group, into both.
2. Compare the answers: what matches, what does not, what is missing from each.
3. Ask one of them to critique the other’s answer.

**Expected result:** they agree on the general points and differ on details; the details where they differ are the ones to check.
**If it fails:** use the “Which one to use for what” table on the page.
**Key message:** no chatbot is best for everything; comparing them is a cheap way to check.

## Instructions and memory

### Custom instructions
Who you are, what you teach, tone and format. They apply to every conversation.

### Memory
Remembers between conversations. It personalises, but carries over context from other topics.

Notas:
To prepare materials for other groups or to see how it answers “from scratch”: a temporary chat or memory turned off. They all let you review and delete what they remember.

---

<!-- .slide: id="temporales" class="table" -->
## Temporary chats

| Chatbot | How long it is kept |
| --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | Up to 30 days, for safety purposes |
| Gemini | 72 hours |
| Claude (incognito) | Not saved in your history |

They do not appear in your history, do not create memories and do not train models.

<!-- .slide: id="entrenamiento" class="table dense" -->
## Do your conversations train models?

| Chatbot | Where to turn it off |
| --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | Data controls → “Improve the model for everyone” |
| Gemini | “Keep Activity”; humans review a portion |
| Claude | Privacy settings → model improvement |
| DeepSeek | Trains on your data and stores it in China |

Notas:
By default, in personal accounts, yes. Organisation accounts usually exclude it by default (for example, Claude’s Team plan). In Gemini, conversations reviewed by people are kept for up to three years.

## Not even a temporary chat makes it acceptable to enter student data

It is kept on the company’s servers for that period.

Notas:
**Demo (5 min): where the settings are**. ChatGPT and Gemini with a test account.

1. Open the settings and show the custom instructions.
2. Show the memory: what it remembers and how to delete it.
3. Start a temporary chat.
4. Show the training setting (“Improve the model for everyone”, “Keep Activity”).

**Expected result:** the group finds the four settings in their own chatbot.
**If it fails:** the paths are in the tables on the page.
**Key message:** five minutes of setting up change the quality of the answers and what is done with your data.

<!-- .slide: id="contexto" -->
## The context window in practice

- With long documents, ask it to cite the page or section, and check it.
- In very long conversations it loses instructions: open a new chat with a summary.
- For many of your own documents, a notebook or a project is better.

## Searching or researching in depth

### Web search
A few queries and an answer with sources. Fast, for specific questions.

### Deep research
An agent that works for several minutes: it plans, searches dozens of sources and writes a referenced report.

Notas:
Deep research is available in ChatGPT, Gemini (including the free plan), Claude (paid plans) and Perplexity. Read the report with a critical eye: open the sources of whatever you are going to use.

---

<!-- .slide: id="prompt-investigacion" class="prompt dense" -->
## A research brief

```text
Write an up-to-date, evidence-based report on strategies for integrating AI into
vocational education and training, for teachers of commerce and marketing.
Include uses with examples, materials for Moodle, documented cases and risks.
Distinguish what is backed by studies from what is a proposal.
Cite the sources for each claim and point out the ones you could not verify.
```

Notas:
The full version is on the page. What matters: as specific as a brief, and ask it to separate evidence from proposals.

<!-- .slide: id="imagenes" -->
## Images: what still goes wrong

- Precise details: clocks, numbers, maps, hands, long text.
- Quantities and relative positions.
- Stereotypes: without instructions, whatever is most frequent in the data.
- ChatGPT and Gemini mark their images as AI-generated.

<!-- .slide: id="documentos" class="checklist" -->
## Getting text into Word or Docs without breaking it

- [ ] Ask for Markdown and paste it where it is understood (Google Docs, with Markdown turned on).
- [ ] Ask for the file directly (ChatGPT and Claude create Word documents, spreadsheets and PDFs).
- [ ] Work on the text in the canvas (Canvas, Artifacts) before exporting.
- [ ] Content first, format afterwards.
- [ ] For formulas, ask for your destination’s notation.

---

<!-- .slide: id="recordar" class="checklist" -->
## Always

- [ ] Plausible is not true: check it at the source.
- [ ] If it matters, regenerate or compare.
- [ ] They tend to agree with you: ask for explicit criticism.
- [ ] No personal data of students in personal accounts.
