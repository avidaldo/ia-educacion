Course materials on **artificial intelligence for secondary and vocational (VET) teachers**: what these tools are and how they work, how to make good use of them when preparing lessons and assessing, and which ethical and legal limits apply.

Each module has a reference page and, in most cases, a **slide deck** with the script of the demonstrations. The decks can be combined to prepare a session with only the topics a group needs.

This English edition covers the law of the European Union: the General Data Protection Regulation and the AI Act. Each country adds its own rules on top of them, such as the age from which a child’s own consent is enough for an online service to process their data, or the tools a school may use with student data, so check them with your education authority before applying a page.
